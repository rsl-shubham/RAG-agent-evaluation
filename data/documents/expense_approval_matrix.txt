ACME TECHNOLOGIES - FINANCIAL DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY (DoA) MATRIX

1. Purpose and Governance
This matrix governs expenditure approvals across operational expenses, travel, software procurement, and capital acquisitions. Any split purchases designed to bypass approval thresholds are considered a financial compliance violation.

2. Delegation Approval Tiers (Operational Expenses & Invoices)
- Tier 1: Up to INR 25,000 (~$300 USD)
  * Approver: Direct Team Lead / Engineering Manager.
- Tier 2: INR 25,001 to INR 1,00,000 (~$1,200 USD)
  * Approver: Department Director / Head of Function.
- Tier 3: INR 1,00,001 to INR 5,00,000 (~$6,000 USD)
  * Approver: Functional Vice President (VP) + Finance Controller.
- Tier 4: INR 5,00,001 to INR 25,00,000 (~$30,000 USD)
  * Approver: Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
- Tier 5: Greater than INR 25,00,000
  * Approver: Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Board Audit Committee notification.

3. Software Subscriptions and Cloud Services
- Any SaaS license or software subscription exceeding INR 50,000 annually requires IT Security review and Procurement approval regardless of budgetary authority tier.
- AWS/GCP cloud resource provisioning exceeding budgeted monthly baseline by more than 15% requires immediate VP of Engineering and CFO approval.

4. Emergency Approvals and Delegation
- During manager absence exceeding 5 business days, formal delegation in the ERP system must be assigned to an equivalent or higher-level peer.
- Post-facto approvals are restricted to Priority 1 production outage incident remediation costs up to INR 2,00,000 with notice sent to the CFO within 24 hours.
