ACME TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE SEPARATION & EXIT POLICY

1. Voluntary Resignation and Notice Period
- Confirmed regular employees must provide a mandatory notice period of 60 calendar days upon submitting voluntary resignation via the HR portal.
- Employees currently undergoing their initial 90-day probationary period are subject to a 30-calendar-day notice period.
- Notice period buy-out (shortening notice in lieu of salary recovery) is subject to mutual written agreement and department Vice President authorization.

2. Handover and Knowledge Transfer (KT)
- The resigning employee must prepare a detailed Knowledge Transfer (KT) document covering current codebases, architectural designs, administrative credentials, and active project deliverables.
- The reporting manager must sign off on the formal KT completion checklist at least 5 business days before the final working day.

3. Company Asset Return and IT Offboarding
- All company-provided equipment (MacBook/laptop, monitors, security badges, access cards, corporate credit cards) must be returned to IT Facilities on or before 4:00 PM on the employee's last working day.
- Single Sign-On (SSO) and VPN credentials will be automatically revoked at 6:00 PM local time on the final working day.

4. Full and Final Settlement (FnF)
- The Full and Final settlement (FnF), including final month prorated salary, encashment of eligible unused annual leave balance (up to 5 carried-forward days max), and gratuity (if eligible after 5 years continuous service), will be disbursed within 30 to 45 calendar days following the last working day.
- Relieving letter and service certificate are released via email within 5 business days following receipt of all asset clearances.
