ACME TECHNOLOGIES - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

1. Password and Authentication Standards
- Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters in length and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Passwords cannot match any of the previous 8 used passwords.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via hardware security key (YubiKey) or approved authenticator apps (Okta Verify, Google Authenticator) is strictly mandatory for all systems. SMS-based MFA is phased out and unpermitted.

2. Data Classification Levels
- Public: Information freely shareable externally (marketing collateral, public press releases).
- Internal: General internal business communications, team wikis, non-confidential project briefs.
- Confidential: Proprietary source code, customer contracts, roadmap documentation, financial reports.
- Restricted: Sensitive customer personal data (PII), payroll databases, encryption keys, board deliberations. Restricted data requires role-based access control and end-to-end encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

3. Device Security, Storage, and BYOD Constraints
- Full disk encryption (FileVault for macOS, BitLocker for Windows) is mandated and enforced via MDM (Jamf / Microsoft Intune).
- Storing unencrypted sensitive or confidential company data on USB flash drives or personal external media is strictly prohibited.
- Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) phones accessing corporate email or Slack must enroll in lightweight Mobile Device Management (MDM) with remote wipe capability for corporate containers.

4. Phishing Reporting and Incident Management
- Employees must report suspicious emails immediately by clicking the 'PhishAlarm' button in Google Workspace or forwarding to security@acmetechnologies.com.
- In case of a suspected breach, device theft, or credential compromise, the employee must contact the Security Incident Response Team (SIRT) hotline immediately at ext. 9111.
