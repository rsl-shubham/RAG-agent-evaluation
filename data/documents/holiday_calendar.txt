ACME TECHNOLOGIES - 2026 OFFICIAL HOLIDAY CALENDAR

1. Standard Mandatory Paid Holidays (All India Locations)
Acme Technologies observes 10 mandatory paid public holidays across all national development centers:
1. New Year's Day - Thursday, January 1, 2026
2. Republic Day - Monday, January 26, 2026
3. Holi - Wednesday, March 4, 2026
4. Good Friday - Friday, April 3, 2026
5. May Day / Labor Day - Friday, May 1, 2026
6. Independence Day - Saturday, August 15, 2026 (Compensatory day off observed Friday, August 14)
7. Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti - Friday, October 2, 2026
8. Dussehra (Vijayadashami) - Tuesday, October 20, 2026
9. Diwali (Deepavali) - Sunday, November 8, 2026 (Additional holiday observed Monday, November 9)
10. Christmas Day - Friday, December 25, 2026

2. Optional / Floating Holidays
- Full-time regular employees are entitled to choose up to 2 Optional / Floating Holidays per calendar year from the approved list of regional observances:
  * Pongal / Makar Sankranti - Wednesday, January 14, 2026
  * Eid-ul-Fitr - Friday, March 20, 2026
  * Raksha Bandhan - Friday, August 28, 2026
  * Ganesh Chaturthi - Monday, September 14, 2026
  * Guru Nanak Jayanti - Tuesday, November 24, 2026
- Optional holidays must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance via the HR Portal and are subject to project staffing coverage.
- Floating holidays cannot be carried over to the following year or encashed.
