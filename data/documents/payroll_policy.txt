ACME TECHNOLOGIES - PAYROLL AND SALARY POLICY

1. Salary Disbursement Schedule
- Monthly salaries are credited directly to employees' designated direct deposit bank accounts on the last business day of each calendar month.
- If the last calendar day falls on a weekend or gazetted national banking holiday, salary disbursement is executed on the preceding business day.

2. Payslips and Tax Deductions
- Digital payslips are generated and accessible via the Acme HR Payroll Portal (ADP self-service) 24 hours prior to pay disbursement.
- Statutory deductions, including Provident Fund (PF), Professional Tax, and Income Tax (TDS), are deducted in accordance with applicable tax regulations.
- Employees must submit their annual tax declaration and investment proofs through the payroll portal before January 15th to avoid higher withholding in the final quarter.

3. Salary Correction Requests and Discrepancies
- Any discrepancies regarding base pay, allowances, or incorrect deductions must be flagged to payroll@acmetechnologies.com within 5 business days of payslip publication.
- Validated underpayments exceeding INR 5,000 are disbursed within an off-cycle payroll run within 3 business days; smaller corrections are adjusted in the subsequent payroll cycle.

4. Performance Bonuses and Variable Compensation
- Annual performance bonuses are evaluated following the Q4 performance cycle and disbursed in the March payroll run.
- To be eligible for bonus payout, the employee must be actively employed on the date of disbursement and must not be serving a formal notice period.

5. Tax Withholding Statements (Form 16)
- Annual Form 16 / Tax Withholding Certificates are issued digitally via the portal by May 31st following the close of the financial year.
