# Employee Onboarding, Equipment Provisioning, and Orientation

ACME TECHNOLOGIES - NEW HIRE ONBOARDING POLICY

1. Pre-Joining Documentation and Background Verification
- Selected candidates must complete digital onboarding in the Acme Candidate Portal at least 7 days before their designated start date.
- Required documents include government photo identification (Passport/PAN/National ID), educational transcripts, previous employment relieving letters, and emergency contact details.
- Background verification (BGV) checks are conducted through a verified third-party agency. Offers are contingent upon satisfactory background clearance.

2. Equipment and Credential Provisioning
- IT automates account generation (Google Workspace, Slack, Jira, GitHub) 3 business days before joining.
- Provisioned laptop and starter kit are dispatched to arrive at the employee's residential address 1 to 2 business days before the joining date.
- Initial single sign-on (SSO) temporary passwords expire within 24 hours of generation and must be reset on day one.

3. First Week Orientation Schedule
- Day 1: Welcome address by HR, company culture overview, benefits briefing, and IT device enrollment.
- Day 2: Mandatory Compliance and Infosec training completion (must be completed within the first 14 days).
- Day 3 to 5: Departmental induction, team introductions, onboarding buddy assignment, and development environment setup.

4. Onboarding Buddy Program
- Each new hire is paired with an experienced peer 'Onboarding Buddy' for their first 60 days to assist with day-to-day tooling, office culture, and informal questions.

5. Probation Period and Confirmation
- Standard probationary period for all newly hired employees is 90 calendar days.
- A 45-day mid-probation review and an 85-day final confirmation review are held between the employee and their reporting manager.
- Upon successful confirmation, formal confirmation letters are issued via the HR portal.
