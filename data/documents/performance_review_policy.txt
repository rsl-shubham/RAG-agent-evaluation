ACME TECHNOLOGIES - PERFORMANCE REVIEW & GROWTH POLICY

1. Review Cadence and Timeline
- Acme Technologies operates on a bi-annual performance evaluation cadence:
  * Mid-Year Check-in (H1): June - July (focusing on goal progress and qualitative coaching; no rating or compensation linkage).
  * Annual Performance Review (H2): December - January (comprehensive evaluation determining merit increases, equity refreshes, and promotions).
- Goal setting (Objectives and Key Results - OKRs) must be finalized in the performance system by the end of January for H1 and July for H2.

2. Evaluation Components and 360 Feedback
- Self-Evaluation: Employee completes self-reflection against assigned OKRs and Acme Core Values.
- Peer Feedback: Employees nominate 3 to 5 cross-functional peers to provide 360-degree feedback.
- Manager Assessment: The direct manager synthesizes OKR metrics, peer reviews, and core behavioral expectations.

3. Performance Rating Scale
- Level 5 (Exceptional Impact): Consistently exceeds all expectations; top ~10% tier.
- Level 4 (Strong Impact): Exceeds targets across primary objectives; ~25% tier.
- Level 3 (Consistent Impact): Meets all performance expectations reliably; ~50% tier.
- Level 2 (Needs Improvement): Inconsistent performance or missed critical milestones; ~10% tier.
- Level 1 (Unsatisfactory Impact): Substantially fails to meet baseline requirements; ~5% tier.

4. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
- Employees receiving a Level 2 or Level 1 rating may be placed on a formal 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- The PIP outlines measurable weekly milestones, remedial training support, and weekly progress check-ins with HR and the manager.
- If acceptable performance is not attained by day 60, employment may be terminated subject to severance and notice guidelines.
