ACME TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE BENEFITS & WELLNESS POLICY

1. Comprehensive Group Medical and Life Insurance
- Acme Technologies covers 100% of the premium for Group Medical Insurance (GMI) providing INR 5,00,000 coverage per year for the employee, spouse, and up to 2 dependent children.
- Optional parental coverage (parents or parents-in-law) can be added during the annual enrollment window in November with a subsidized co-pay.
- Group Term Life Insurance (GTLI) provides death benefit coverage equal to 3x the employee's Annual Base Salary.
- Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) covers up to INR 25,00,000 in case of permanent disability.

2. Professional Learning and Development (L&D) Budget
- Every regular full-time employee is entitled to an annual individual Learning & Development allowance of INR 15,000 per calendar year.
- Eligible expenses include technical certifications (AWS, GCP, PMP, etc.), industry conferences, educational subscriptions (Coursera, O'Reilly), and books.
- Pre-approval from the direct manager is mandatory via the Acme Learning Portal before purchasing any course or exam voucher.
- Reimbursements must be claimed within 30 days of completion along with passing certificate/receipt.

3. Home Internet and Mobile Connectivity Allowance
- Remote and hybrid employees receive an Internet Reimbursement allowance of up to INR 1,500 per month for home broadband connectivity.
- Employees whose role requires regular customer contact or emergency on-call duty receive an additional mobile plan allowance of up to INR 1,000 per month upon manager endorsement.

4. IT Hardware Allocation and Ergonomic Setup
- Every full-time employee is provisioned a company laptop (Apple MacBook Pro 14" or Dell Latitude 5540, depending on departmental role).
- An initial one-time Ergonomic Home Office Setup allowance of INR 10,000 is available for new hires to purchase an external monitor, ergonomic chair, or standing desk converter. Invoices must be submitted within 60 days of joining.

5. Health, Wellness, and Mental Health
- All employees and their dependents have free, confidential 24/7 access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) including up to 8 free therapy sessions per year.
- Annual Gym or Fitness Membership reimbursement of up to INR 6,000 per year is claimable in two half-yearly installments.
