EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY

1. Health Insurance:
The company provides health insurance coverage of
INR 5,00,000 per employee per year.

2. Internet Reimbursement:
Employees working remotely can claim internet expenses
up to INR 1,000 per month.

3. Learning Budget:
Employees receive an annual learning and development
budget of INR 15,000.

4. Laptop:
The company provides a laptop to every full-time employee.
