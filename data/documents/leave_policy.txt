COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave:
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave:
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

3. Casual Leave:
Employees receive 5 days of casual leave per calendar year.

4. Leave Approval:
Employees must submit leave requests through the HR portal.
Manager approval is required before taking planned leave.

5. Carry Forward:
A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried
forward to the next calendar year.
