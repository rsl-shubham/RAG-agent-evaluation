ACME TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE LEAVE AND TIME-OFF POLICY

1. Overview and Eligibility
This policy governs all full-time and part-time salaried employees at Acme Technologies. Contractors and temporary personnel are not eligible for paid leave under this policy.

2. Annual Paid Leave
- Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year (accrued at 1.67 days per completed month of service).
- Annual leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days require submission at least 10 business days in advance via the Acme HR Portal (Workday).
- Manager approval is required before taking planned annual leave.
- Unplanned annual leave is not permitted except in documented emergency situations.

3. Sick and Medical Leave
- Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, credited in full on January 1st.
- Sick leave is intended for employee illness, injury, or medical appointments.
- For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate signed by a licensed healthcare professional is mandatory upon return.
- Unused sick leave lapses on December 31st each year and cannot be cashed out or carried forward.

4. Casual Leave
- Employees receive 5 days of casual leave per calendar year for urgent personal matters.
- Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave or public holidays without prior written approval from the Department Head.
- Maximum continuous casual leave allowed is 2 days at a time.

5. Parental Leave (Maternity and Paternity)
- Maternity Leave: Eligible female employees who have completed at least 80 days of continuous service at Acme Technologies are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children. For third child onwards, maternity leave is 12 weeks.
- Paternity Leave: Eligible male or secondary caregivers are entitled to 10 working days of fully paid paternity leave, which must be availed within 6 months of the child's birth or legal adoption.
- Adoption Leave: Primary adoptive parents are entitled to 12 weeks of paid leave for adopting a child below the age of 1 year.

6. Bereavement Leave
- Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive paid working days in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- For extended family members (grandparents, in-laws), up to 3 consecutive paid working days are granted.

7. Leave Carry Forward and Encashment
- A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the following calendar year.
- Any annual leave balance exceeding 5 days at midnight on December 31st is automatically forfeited.
- Leave encashment is permitted only upon formal separation from Acme Technologies as part of the full and final settlement. Casual and sick leaves are strictly non-encashable.

8. Unpaid Leave of Absence
- Sabbatical or unpaid personal leave exceeding 30 calendar days requires approval from the Chief People Officer (CPO) and the respective Business Unit Head.
