ACME TECHNOLOGIES - IT SUPPORT & WORKPLACE ASSETS POLICY

1. IT Service Desk Channels and Operating Hours
- IT Helpdesk operates 24x7 for P1 critical outages and Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM for standard requests.
- All requests must be submitted through the Jira Service Management portal (https://servicedesk.acme.internal) or via the Slack channel #it-helpdesk.
- Urgent hardware replacement issues can be escalated by calling the IT Emergency Hotline at ext. 4357 (HELP).

2. Laptop Allocation, Refresh, and Replacement
- Engineering & Design: 16-inch or 14-inch Apple MacBook Pro M-series with 32GB RAM.
- Business, Sales & Operations: Dell Latitude 5540 (Intel Core i7, 16GB RAM) or Lenovo ThinkPad T14.
- Standard hardware refresh cycle is 36 months (3 years). Premature replacements require Director of IT authorization.
- In the event of accidental damage or hardware malfunction, a loaner laptop is dispatched within 24 business hours.

3. Remote Access and Virtual Private Network (VPN)
- Corporate network access requires the Acme Secure GlobalProtect VPN with mandatory multi-factor authentication (MFA via Duo Mobile / Okta Verify).
- Direct internet access to internal production servers, staging clusters, and databases without active VPN tunneling is technically prohibited.
- Split-tunneling is enabled strictly for recognized video conferencing domains (Zoom, Google Meet) to optimize bandwidth.

4. Software Installation and Administrative Privileges
- Standard administrative privileges on corporate machines are restricted by default.
- Developers needing local root/admin rights must request Privileged Access Management (PAM) approval via the IT portal, valid for renewable 6-month intervals.
- Installing unapproved software, peer-to-peer applications, or commercial licenses without procurement sign-off is a violation of company policy.

5. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for IT Tickets
- Priority 1 (System Outage / Blocker): Response within 15 minutes, resolution target 2 hours.
- Priority 2 (High / Single User Blocker): Response within 1 hour, resolution target 8 hours.
- Priority 3 (Medium / Standard Request): Response within 4 hours, resolution target 24-48 hours.
- Priority 4 (Low / General Inquiry / Enhancement): Response within 24 hours, resolution target 5 business days.
